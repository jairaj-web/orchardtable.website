The Gluten-Free Bread Boutique

Baked With Love, Milled By Hand

Everything here is made with organic, non-GMO almond, coconut & seed flours — and much of our fruit, vegetables & herbs are grown right on our farm. All-natural & preservative-free: we mill our flour ourselves, build our own gluten-free sourdough starters, and sweeten only with coconut sugar, jaggery & coconut oil — never processed sugars or fats.
