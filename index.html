From Orchard Table

A Culinary Oasis in the City

Nestled amidst lush greenery and birdsong on Yashbans Road, ORCHARD TABLE invites you to indulge in a culinary oasis that brings together wholesome organic ingredients from our on-site kitchen garden.

As you step into this urban farm, serenity engulfs your senses, creating an escape from the bustling city life. Savor the delectable flavors of meticulously crafted dishes while your children connect with nature, visit our happy farm animals & enjoy the outdoors.
